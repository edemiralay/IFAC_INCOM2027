# IFAC_INCOM2027
This repository provides supplementary materials for the conference paper “A Conceptual Framework for Human–Robot Task Assignment in Collaborative Manufacturing.”

The study investigates characteristics relevant to human–robot task assignment through a systematic literature review (SLR). The identified characteristics are organized into three principal domains: Task, Robot, and Human.

Based on the literature synthesis, a four-stage conceptual framework is proposed, consisting of characterization, task–resource compatibility assessment, task assignment, and performance evaluation.

**Supplementary Materials**

The repository is intended to provide:

  **1. Search Strategy**: Scopus search expressions, search dates, and eligibility criteria.

  **2. Publication Selection**: Bibliographic records and documented inclusion and exclusion decisions.

  **3. Characteristics Taxonomy**: Definitions and classification of task requirements, robot capabilities, and human characteristics.

  **4. Literature Evidence Matrix**: Relationships between the reviewed publications, identified characteristics, and their roles in task-assignment approaches.

**Literature Review**

The Scopus searches initially identified 1,676 records. After duplicate removal, language and document-type restrictions, and title-and-abstract screening, 426 publications remained for full-text retrieval. Of these, 412 full texts were accessible.

The manuscript reports 348 included publications following full-text eligibility assessment. The final publication selection register and corresponding exclusion decisions will be made available following verification.

**Data Availability**

The supplementary materials contain bibliographic information, classification records, and literature-based evidence summaries. Full-text publications accessed through institutional subscriptions are not redistributed.
